Test your cycling Bluetooth connection without a smart trainer.

Cadensio turns a compatible Android phone into a simulated Bluetooth LE cycling sensor. Set power and cadence, then connect a cycling app on another device to test data reception.

WHO IS IT FOR?
Developers, technical support teams and users diagnosing an FTMS connection or exploring how cycling apps receive power and cadence data.

CONTROL YOUR TEST SIGNAL
• Set power from 0 to 1,000 W, with quick presets for 100, 150 and 250 W.
• Set cadence from 0 to 200 rpm.
• Adjust power and cadence variation independently, or use zero variation for a constant signal.
• Set simulated speed from 0 to 60 km/h.
• Optional ERG mode follows received FTMS target-power commands. Cadence stays manual.
• See broadcasting, connections and data subscriptions separately.

CONNECT IN THREE STEPS
1. Set your signal.
2. Start Bluetooth broadcasting on your phone.
3. On another device, find your phone's Bluetooth name in your cycling app.

Cadensio broadcasts FTMS, Cycling Power and Cycling Speed and Cadence services. Compatibility depends on your phone and receiving app; universal compatibility is not guaranteed.

SIMPLE AND TRANSPARENT
Free, without ads, in-app purchases or accounts. The app works offline with no tracking SDK. Settings stay on your phone. English and French interface. Source code and support: https://github.com/Altorru/cadensio

BEFORE YOU START
Requires Android 8 or later and a phone supporting Bluetooth LE peripheral advertising. The receiving cycling app must run on another device. A notification lets you stop background broadcasting.

Cadensio is a simulation and diagnostic tool. It does not measure physical effort, control a real trainer or replace an exercise sensor. Do not present synthetic data as a real activity or use it to obtain competition results or rewards. Cadensio is independent of cycling platforms.
