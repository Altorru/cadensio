Connected-device service demonstration

Cadensio 1.0.0 · fr.hugoviaud.cadensio · 16 November 2026

Android emulator screen recording: the user starts Bluetooth advertising, leaves the app, opens the persistent notification, expands it, stops the service with “Arrêter”, then returns to the stopped app. French UI: “Démarrer la diffusion” = Start broadcasting; “Arrêter” = Stop.

The connected-device foreground service maintains a user-started BLE peripheral session. It advertises FTMS, Cycling Power and Cycling Speed/Cadence services and sends synthetic sensor notifications to subscribing receivers. If the service is interrupted in the background, receiver tests lose their continuous sensor stream. Users can stop it from the app or notification. It does not restart automatically.

This recording demonstrates the service lifecycle on an emulator. No physical receiver is connected; it is not evidence of real-device Bluetooth interoperability.
